The Verified [Dashboard](https://dashboard.verified.inc) connects to [Claude](https://claude.ai) as a custom connector, so you can ask about your data in plain language (your signup and verification volumes, trends over time, and recent request logs), without leaving the chat.

:::note Read-only and scoped to you
The connector can **read** your dashboard data but can never change anything, and it only ever sees the brands and data your own Dashboard login can access.
:::

## What you can ask

Once connected, try prompts like:

- "List my brands."
- "How many 1-Click Signups did I have last month, broken down by week?"
- "Compare Text-to-Signup volume this quarter to last quarter."
- "Show me the errored requests for one of my brands over the last 7 days."

Just ask in natural language, Claude figures out the rest.

## Before you start

To connect, you'll need a couple of things in place:

<ol type="a">
    <b><li>A Verified Dashboard account (the login you use at dashboard.verified.inc).</li></b>
    <b><li>A Claude account.</li></b>
</ol>

Don't have a Dashboard account yet?

<HowToCreateADashboardAccount />

You'll also need the connector URL:

```
https://dashboard-mcp.verified.inc/mcp
```

## 1. Add the connector in Claude.

In Claude, open the **+** menu in the message box, then choose **Add connector → Add custom connector**.

![Claude's Add connector menu with Add custom connector highlighted](/img/mcp/select-add-connector.png)

In the dialog:

<ol type="a">
    <b><li>Give it a name, for example "Verified Dashboard".</li></b>
    <b><li>Paste the connector URL: https://dashboard-mcp.verified.inc/mcp</li></b>
    <b><li>Expand Advanced settings and set the Client ID to dashboard-mcp. Leave the OAuth Client Secret blank.</li></b>
    <b><li>Click Add.</li></b>
</ol>

![The Add custom connector dialog filled in with the URL and Client ID](/img/mcp/add-custom-connector.png)

## 2. Sign in and authorize.

Connecting opens a Verified page in your browser.

<ol type="a">
    <b><li>Click Sign in and sign in with your usual Verified Dashboard credentials (including 2FA or SSO if your account uses them).</li></b>
</ol>

![Sign in to continue screen](/img/mcp/sign-in-to-dashboard.png)

<ol type="a" start="2">
    <b><li>On the next screen, review the access being requested and click Approve. You'll be returned to Claude automatically.</li></b>
</ol>

![Authorize Verified Dashboard screen with Approve and Deny buttons](/img/mcp/authorize-mcp.png)

## 3. Confirm it's available.

Open the **+** menu again and go to **Connectors**. You should see **Verified Dashboard** listed and toggled on.

![Connectors menu showing Verified Dashboard enabled](/img/mcp/confirm-its-available.png)

**That's it! Start asking Claude about your dashboard. ✅**

## What Claude can and can't see

<ul>
    <li><b>Read-only.</b> The connector can't create, change, or delete anything in your dashboard.</li>
    <li><b>Your data only.</b> Requests run with your own permissions, so you only ever see the brands and data your account can already access.</li>
    <li><b>No personal data in logs.</b> Request logs leave out end-user personal information, Claude sees operational details like timestamps, products, status codes, and error codes, but not the personal data behind them.</li>
    <li><b>Live production data.</b> The connector reports on your production environment.</li>
</ul>

## Managing the connector

You can turn the connector off or remove it at any time from the **Connectors** menu in Claude (**Manage connectors**). Access is also tied to your Verified session: if your session ends or your account needs additional verification, the connector stops returning data until you sign in again.

## Troubleshooting

:::tip
Most connection issues come down to the URL or the Client ID.
:::

<ul>
    <li><b>It won't connect, or keeps asking you to sign in:</b> make sure the URL ends in <code>/mcp</code> and the Client ID is exactly <code>dashboard-mcp</code> with no client secret.</li>
    <li><b>Connected, but no data:</b> confirm you signed in to the right Verified account and that it has activity in the date range you asked about.</li>
    <li><b>A date range looks empty:</b> try a wider range, a brand with no activity in that window returns nothing.</li>
</ul>
